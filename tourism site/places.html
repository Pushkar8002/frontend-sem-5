Popular Places in Delhi

India Gate

India Gate is a famous war memorial located in the heart of Delhi.

Red Fort

Red Fort is a historic monument built during the Mughal period.

Qutub Minar

Qutub Minar is a famous historical tower and one of Delhi's most popular tourist attractions.

Lotus Temple

Lotus Temple is famous for its beautiful lotus-shaped architecture.

Humayun's Tomb

Humayun's Tomb is a beautiful historical monument and an important example of Mughal architecture.
