Red Fort

The Red Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in Delhi, India. It was built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1638 and served as the main residence of the Mughal emperors for nearly 200 years. The fort is known for its stunning architecture and historical significance.

India Gate

India Gate is a war memorial located in New Delhi, India. It was built in 1931 and is dedicated to the soldiers of the British Indian Army who died in World War I.

Kutub Minar

Kutub Minar is a tall brick minaret located in Delhi, India. It was built in 1193 by Qutb-ud-din Aibak and is a great example of Indo-Islamic architecture.

Gateway of India

The Gateway of India is a famous monument built in Mumbai in 1924. It was built to welcome King George V and Queen Mary and overlooks the Arabian Sea.

Visit Guidelines
