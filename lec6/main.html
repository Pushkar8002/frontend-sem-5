Famous Cities of India

Lucknow

Lucknow is the capital of Uttar Pradesh. It is famous for its history, culture, architecture, kebabs and biryani.

Agra

Agra is famous for the Taj Mahal. It is also known for its historical buildings, carpets and handicrafts.

Patna

Patna is the capital of Bihar. It is known for its history, culture, Patna Museum and Golghar.

Muzaffarpur

Muzaffarpur is famous for Shahi Litchi and is an important commercial city of North Bihar.
