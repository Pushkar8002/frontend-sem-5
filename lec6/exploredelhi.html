Welcome to Delhi

Delhi is the capital of India and one of the most historic cities in the country. It is famous for its monuments, culture, food and markets.

Places to Visit in Delhi

India Gate

India Gate is a famous war memorial and one of the most popular tourist attractions in Delhi.

Red Fort

The Red Fort is a historic monument built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Qutub Minar

Qutub Minar is one of the most famous historical monuments in Delhi and is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Lotus Temple

Lotus Temple is famous for its unique lotus-shaped architecture and peaceful surroundings.

Famous Food of Delhi

  • Butter Chicken
  • Paratha

  • Jalebi
